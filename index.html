Common Questions

Are your mangoes carbide-free?

Yes, 100%. We strictly prohibit the use of Calcium Carbide or any artificial ripening agents. Our mangoes mature naturally on the tree and finish ripening in safe, traditional hay beds.

How long does delivery take?

Typically, orders are processed and shipped within 24 hours of harvesting. Depending on your location, it takes 1-3 business days. The box represents the true farm-to-table timeline!

How should I store them when they arrive?

If they are firm, keep them at room temperature until they yield slightly to pressure and smell fragrant. Once ripe, you can store them in the refrigerator to prolong their freshness.
